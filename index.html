About the Story

When We Meet Again is an emotional journey about friendship, love, heartbreak, and destiny. What begins as a chance meeting between two strangers on a train slowly unfolds into a story filled with memories, sacrifices, unexpected reunions, and the hope that some bonds can never truly be broken.

Meet the Characters

Siddhartha (Siddu)

A kind-hearted engineering student whose life changes after a chance encounter and an unforgettable first love.

Sneha Deepika

A compassionate stranger whose curiosity leads her into Siddhu's past, uncovering memories that change both their lives.
